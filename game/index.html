Costa Alta · sun, salt & bad decisions

Cole Maddox just walked out of Costa Alta Correctional after three years. His partner Lena Cruz is waiting at the gate with a borrowed car and a plan. Rent is due, Felix needs a favour, and one easy score could set them free — or put them both back inside.

WASD / arrows — move & drive · E — enter / steal car · Mouse — aim, click — shoot
Q — swap Cole / Lena · R — radio · P / Esc — pause · Touch: left stick + buttons

An original game. No real places, people or brands. All art and sound made in code.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Move: WASD / arrows. On foot, walk up to any parked car and press E to take it.
  • Drive: W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A / D steer. Cars are faster — and louder.
  • Fight: buy a pistol at Steel & Smoke Pawn. Aim with the mouse, click to shoot.
  • Wanted: stealing, hitting people and shooting raise your stars. 1 star: one patrol car. 3 stars: roadblocks and a police helicopter. 5 stars: the whole city is after you.
  • Lose the heat: break line of sight, hide in alleys, wait out the cooldown.
  • Busted: if a cop pins you down, you lose some cash and wake up at the safehouse.
  • Missions: follow the yellow marker. Talk to Felix, do the jobs, bank the cash.
  • Swap: press Q to switch between Cole and Lena. Cash and heat are shared.
